About

The grid

Vnua comes from the letters of my last name, Ventura. I came up with it in eighth grade. The mark is a 3×3 grid, like one face of a puzzle cube, and it's on everything Vnua Industries makes.

Vnua Apparel puts that grid on clothes. Drop 01 gives each piece one of the cube's six colors.

Who's behind it

I'm Adriel Ventura, a mechanical engineering student at Hofstra. Vnua Apparel is one part of my company, next to the software and engineering work.
