Club drops

I help lead Hofstra’s SHPE chapter and AIAA branch. Club merch usually means someone fronting money for a box of shirts in sizes nobody wanted. Preorders fix that: members vote, people order what they’ll wear, and the club keeps what it raises.

The grid

Vnua comes from the letters of my last name, Ventura. I came up with it in eighth grade. The mark is a 3×3 grid, like one face of a puzzle cube, and it’s on everything Vnua Industries makes. Drop 01 gives each piece one of the cube’s six colors.

Who’s behind it

I’m Adriel Ventura, a mechanical engineering student at Hofstra. Vnua Apparel sits next to Vnua Systems, my company’s defense manufacturing work.
