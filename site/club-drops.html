SHPE and AIAA

Preorder merch for two Hofstra engineering clubs. Each club runs its own campaign and keeps the money. Nothing is on sale yet.

01 / Where it stands

Four steps, no inventory.

Nothing gets printed until people have ordered it, and nothing goes on sale until Hofstra approves it.

  1. Now

    Members vote

    Each club picks between design concepts like the ones below.

  2. Next

    Hofstra approval

    The club files Hofstra’s trademark use request with a mockup and orders only from an approved vendor.[3]

  3. Then

    Preorder

    A Custom Ink Fundraising page, open for about two weeks. No upfront cost to the club.[1]

  4. Last

    Print and pick up

    If the print minimum is met, it prints. If not, buyers are refunded.[2]

03 / How the money works

The club keeps it.

  • No upfront cost. Buyers pay on the preorder page; the club doesn’t buy inventory.[1]
  • No leftovers. If a design doesn’t reach its print minimum, buyers are refunded and nothing is made.[2] Nobody on the e-board pays to cover the gap.
  • The club is the beneficiary. Each campaign is set up by the club, for the club. Vnua Apparel only hosts this page and takes nothing.
  • Approved first. Anything with a Hofstra club’s name needs written approval from the club’s program office before it goes to a vendor.[3]
